# Open questions for the owner

Facts that are not in the repo. Cowork should leave a visible placeholder (for example `[PRICE TBD]`) until these are answered.

1. **Contact and call to action:** email, phone, website or booking link to put on the last slide and the end card.
2. **Pricing:** is there a price, a free trial or a pilot offer? If not, leave pricing out.
3. **Launch date and place:** is there a date, an event or a channel (website, WhatsApp groups, YouTube, Instagram)?
4. **Presenter:** name, title and photo for slide 1.
5. **Logo file:** is there a formal logo? If not, use the wave glyph plus the name.
6. **Real references:** any real club, coach quote or number that may be used? Until then, use only the labelled demo data.
7. **Languages:** English only, or also Hindi or another language for voice-over and captions?
8. **Roadmap:** anything planned that may be shown? Examples the owner might add: parent view, dark theme, edit profile, fee tracking. Do not show unannounced items.
9. **Public hosting:** the production site is behind a password. Is a public demo link wanted? If yes, say where.
10. **Music and voice:** licensed music or an AI voice, and which accent.
