## Feature blurbs (about 20 words each)
- **Sessions.** Start the morning or evening session in one tap. Everyone begins as present. You only mark who is missing.
- **Timings.** Choose the stroke and distance once. Type each swimmer's time, press Enter, move to the next.
- **Personal bests.** The app spots a new personal best the moment you save the time and shows how much faster it was.
- **Students.** Exact age by the day, parent contacts and coach notes for every swimmer, in one list.
- **Progress report.** Attendance, streak and personal bests for each swimmer, ready to show a parent or print.
- **Performance.** A trend chart for each stroke and distance, with the personal best on every chip, so you can see whether training is working.
- **Coach notes and skill ratings.** Rate each stroke from Beginner to Elite and keep short notes tagged technique, fitness, behavior or goal. The latest notes are on the swimmer's page when you need them.
- **Clubs.** Every club has its own private data. The head coach adds assistants and can switch them off at any time.
- **Phone first.** Add it to your home screen. It opens like an app and fits the way you stand at the pool.

## Objection handling
- **"Do we need the internet?"** Yes. That keeps everyone's data in one place and never out of sync. Pool decks with a phone signal are all you need.
- **"Do parents need an app?"** No. Coaches stay in control, and parents are one tap away by call, WhatsApp or text.
- **"Is our data private?"** Each club's data is in its own database file, and only that club's coaches can open it.
- **"Is it hard to learn?"** There are four tabs. A new assistant coach can start a session on the first day.
