# Launch video script

Length: 50 to 60 seconds. Formats: 16:9 for the website and 9:16 (vertical) for social. Voice: a calm, friendly coach. Music: light, rhythmic and upbeat, about 100 to 110 beats per minute, with a steady pulse that matches the name Cadence. No lyrics. Keep the music under the voice.

Brand rules: [brand-and-style.md](brand-and-style.md). Screens: [screenshots/](screenshots/) or fresh recordings from [demo-walkthrough.md](demo-walkthrough.md). Label demo data on screen in small text: "Demo data".

## Voice-over (full text, about 100 words)
Times on your hand. Attendance in a chat group. There is a better way to run the pool deck. This is Cadence Lanes. Start a session in one tap. Everyone is present. You mark only who is missing. Pick the stroke once. Type each time. If it is a personal best, you know at once. Every swimmer, every parent, one tap away. Attendance and personal bests for every swimmer, on one screen. Watch the times drop, stroke by stroke. Keep your notes where you need them. Add it to your home screen and go. Cadence Lanes. Track every lap. Grow every swimmer.

## Motion and sound
- Transitions: quick slides and soft fades, 0.3 s. Lane lines draw left to right to link scenes.
- Tap indicators: a soft violet circle where the thumb touches.
- Sound design: a whistle at 0:02, a light tap sound on each tap, a short rising chime on the personal best, a soft splash at the logo reveal.
- Captions: always on, large, white on forest or forest on sea glass. Many people watch without sound.

## Shorter cut (15 seconds, for social)
"Start a session in one tap." (home and attendance) "Type a time. See the personal best at once." (timings) "Cadence Lanes. Track every lap. Grow every swimmer." (end card)

## Do not
- Do not show real children, real phone numbers or real club names. Use the demo data only.
- Do not claim customers, prices or results.
