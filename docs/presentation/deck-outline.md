# Deck outline (12 to 14 slides)

Slides are numbered 1 to 14. Slides 9 and 10 (performance, notes and ratings) are the value-add; keep them in any short version of the deck.

Format: 16:9. Brand rules in [brand-and-style.md](brand-and-style.md). One idea per slide, big type, real screenshots in a plain phone frame. Speaker notes should be 2 to 4 sentences in the voice of a coach.

Audience: head coaches and academy owners. Goal: they agree to a demo or to register their academy.

| # | Slide | Headline | Content | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Optional extra slides
- **Roadmap.** Only if the owner provides one. Do not invent items.
- **Pricing.** Only if the owner provides it.
- **How it works (technical).** One slide for technical buyers: PWA, FastAPI, SQLite per club, Docker, HTTPS.

## Speaker note style (example for slide 6)
"Choose freestyle and 50 metres once. Now each time you type, you press Enter and move down the list. If a swimmer just beat their best, the app tells you right away, with the seconds saved. That moment at the wall is when the swimmer cares most."
