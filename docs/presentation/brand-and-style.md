# Brand and style

## Name
**Cadence Lanes** (two words, both capitalised). Never "CadenceLanes" in headlines. The SMS sender name is the one-word `CadenceLanes`.

## Tagline
**Track every lap. Grow every swimmer.**

## Look: the "Tidepool" theme
Calm sea-glass greens, deep forest ink, one violet accent. Rounded shapes, pill buttons, a floating bottom bar. It should feel modern and friendly, not clinical.

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Ink and header | Forest | `#10332C` |
| Accent, buttons, highlights | Violet | `#5B3FD6` |
| Page background | Sea glass | `#E8F0EC` |
| Cards | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Soft surfaces | Mist | `#D6E4DD` |
| Muted text | Moss | `#587A70` |
| Success and present | Green | `#1C8657` |
| Error and absent | Red | `#C13E3C` |
| Info | Blue | `#2563EB` |
| Streak flame | Orange | `#F97316` |

A lighter violet `#A18FFF` works on dark forest backgrounds.

## Fonts
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** for everything (headlines in 800 weight, body in 400 to 600).
- **DM Mono** for times and numbers that line up (for example `31.42s`).
Both are free on Google Fonts.

## Logo
The mark is three wavy lane lines (the "waves" glyph) in violet, with the words Cadence Lanes in bold. A formal logo file does not exist yet; build a simple lockup from the glyph and the font above, or see [open-questions.md](open-questions.md).

## Tone of voice
- Warm, direct and practical, like a good coach: short sentences, active voice, no jargon.
- Talk about the pool deck, the whistle, the wall, the clock. Avoid tech words (database, platform, solution).
- Be honest about limits (needs internet, coaches only).
- No emojis. No hype words such as "revolutionary" or "game-changing".

## Imagery
- Use the real phone screenshots in `screenshots/`, inside a plain phone frame.
- Backgrounds: sea glass or forest, with faint lane lines (thin horizontal lines) if decoration is wanted.
- Photography, if used: pool deck, stopwatch, swimmers' hands on the wall, a coach with a phone. Avoid stock photos of boardrooms.

## Do
- Show the product in use at the pool.
- Put one idea on each slide or each video shot.
- Label demo data as "demo data".

## Do not
- Do not show real children's names, faces or phone numbers. The demo names are fictional.
- Do not claim customer numbers, prices, awards or results that are not in [product-brief.md](product-brief.md).
- Do not use emojis.
